About United Recycling

A licensed and bonded Georgia scrap metal company on Hurricane Shoals Road in Lawrenceville.

Who you're dealing with

Straight Weights, Straight Prices

United Recycling Scrap Metals LLC is run by Gus Harvey out of the yard at 621 Hurricane Shoals Rd in Lawrenceville. We buy all ferrous and non-ferrous metals from the public, from contractors, and from industrial plants across North Georgia.

The whole business runs on one idea: pay more than the yard down the road, grade honestly in front of the customer, and answer the phone. That's why most of our volume comes from people who came once and kept coming back.

We are a licensed and bonded company, and we are open to the public.
