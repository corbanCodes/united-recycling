Commercial & Industrial Demolition

Big industrial plants, big commercial plants, schools and mini-malls — torn down, hauled out and paid for. No residential.

This is a service we run, not a sideline

We Tear It Down And Take It Away

United Recycling does demolition. Not just container drops and scrap pickups — actual teardowns. Big industrial plants, big commercial plants, schools, mini-malls, warehouses, processing lines, tank farms and everything bolted to them.

Because we are the metal buyer and the demolition crew, the scrap value comes straight off your project cost instead of disappearing into a middleman's invoice. That is the whole advantage, and it is a big one on a job with real tonnage in it.

Commercial and industrial only. We don't do houses, garages or residential tear-downs — this crew is set up for plants, schools, mini-malls and buildings with real tonnage in them.
